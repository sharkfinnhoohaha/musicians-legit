{{company_legal_name}} ("Company") shall pay {{artist_legal_name}} ("Artist") an advance of ${{advance_amount}} against future Artist royalties (the "Advance"). The Advance and the costs listed below as "Recoupable Costs" are recoverable by Company solely out of Artist's royalty earnings under this Agreement and never as a personal debt.

Recoupable Costs (recovered from Artist royalties):
- Recording costs (studio, engineer, mix, master) approved in writing in advance.
- Producer fees and producer advances.
- Music video budgets approved in writing in advance.
- Independent radio promotion and tour support, only if pre-approved in writing by Artist.

Non-Recoupable Costs (paid by Company, not recoverable from Artist):
- Company overhead, salaries, and general administrative expenses.
- Marketing and publicity activities undertaken by Company's in-house staff.
- Manufacturing, distribution, and digital delivery fees absorbed by Company in the ordinary course of business.
- Legal fees Company incurs to negotiate this Agreement.

If Company seeks to treat any cost as Recoupable that is not listed above, Company must obtain Artist's written approval before incurring that cost.
