Cancellation by Purchaser. If Purchaser cancels the engagement for any reason other than a Force Majeure Event, Purchaser shall pay Artist according to this ladder: {{cancellation_ladder}} (for example, "more than 60 days before the show: deposit forfeited; 30 to 60 days before: 50% of Fee; less than 30 days before: 100% of Fee"). Cancellation does not relieve Purchaser of any expense reimbursement already invoiced.

Cancellation by Artist. If Artist cancels for any reason other than a Force Majeure Event, Artist shall promptly refund any deposit and shall use good-faith efforts to suggest a replacement performer of comparable stature, but is not otherwise liable for consequential damages.

Force Majeure. A "Force Majeure Event" means an event beyond the reasonable control of the affected party that makes performance impossible, illegal, or unsafe, including: {{force_majeure_definition}} (for example, natural disaster, severe weather warning, public-health emergency, government order, war, civil unrest, venue destruction, death or serious illness of Artist or a key Artist Member, or failure of essential utilities at the venue).

If a Force Majeure Event prevents the engagement, neither party is liable for damages, but the deposit shall be returned, less Artist's documented out-of-pocket expenses already incurred for travel, lodging, and production costs that cannot be refunded. The parties shall use good-faith efforts to reschedule on mutually acceptable dates within the next twelve (12) months in lieu of cancellation.
