The parties acknowledge that ownership of a master sound recording (the "Master") in the absence of a specific written agreement typically follows authorship and economic contribution. To avoid ambiguity, the parties confirm the following ownership rule for the Master titled "{{master_title}}".

The party who paid the documented out-of-pocket recording costs (studio, engineer, mix, master, session musicians, and producer fees) for the Master is {{cost_payer_legal_name}} (the "Cost Payer"). The Cost Payer's payment of those costs does not by itself transfer authorship rights of the performing artists or producers, but it is a relevant factor in confirming default ownership.

Default Ownership Election: {{ownership_default}}.

- "ARTIST OWNS" means the performing artist owns 100% of the Master notwithstanding who paid recording costs; any costs advanced by another party are treated as a recoupable advance, not as a transfer of ownership.
- "COST PAYER OWNS" means the Cost Payer owns the Master, and the performing artist receives a royalty under separately documented terms.
- "JOINT OWNERSHIP" means the Master is jointly owned by the listed parties in the percentages stated elsewhere in this Agreement.

If this clause conflicts with a separately signed work-for-hire, recording, or split agreement covering the Master, the more specific agreement controls.
