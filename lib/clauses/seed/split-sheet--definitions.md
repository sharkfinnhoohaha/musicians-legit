This Split Sheet (the "Agreement") is entered into on {{creation_date}} by and between {{party_a_legal_name}} and {{party_b_legal_name}} (each a "Writer" and together the "Writers").

The "Composition" means the musical work currently titled "{{song_working_title}}", including its music and lyrics in their current form and any subsequent revisions made jointly by the Writers.

"Songwriter Share" means each Writer's percentage interest in the underlying musical composition copyright. "Publisher Share" means each Writer's percentage interest in the publishing side of the same composition. Together, the Songwriter Share and Publisher Share for any single Writer represent that Writer's total ownership of the Composition.

The Writers acknowledge that this Agreement covers only the underlying Composition and does not by itself transfer or determine ownership of any sound recording (master) embodying the Composition.
