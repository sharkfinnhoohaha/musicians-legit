Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with any schedules, exhibits, and signed addenda referenced in it, is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter. It replaces any prior or contemporaneous agreement, proposal, or understanding, whether oral or written, on the same subject.

Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by all parties. Conduct, course of dealing, or email exchanges do not amend this Agreement unless they expressly state an intent to amend and are signed by all parties.

Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held by a competent court or arbitrator to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be replaced with a valid provision that most closely reflects the original intent.

Waiver. A party's failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver of that provision or any other provision, and a one-time waiver does not waive future enforcement of the same provision.

Notices. Notices under this Agreement shall be sent in writing to the email addresses on file for each party (Party A: {{notice_email_party_a}}; Party B: {{notice_email_party_b}}) and shall be effective on the date the email is sent, provided no bounce or non-delivery message is received. Either party may update its notice address by written notice to the other.

Counterparts and Electronic Signatures. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute the same agreement.
