Until the Sample is fully cleared in writing, the Company may hold back {{holdback_pct}}% of the royalties otherwise payable to the Artist on recordings containing the Sample (the "Holdback"). The Holdback shall be placed in a clearly identified reserve and disclosed on each royalty statement.

The Holdback is intended to fund any settlement, license fee, or share assignment that may be required to clear the Sample, and to cover documented legal fees directly attributable to clearance. Company shall not use the Holdback for any other purpose.

The Holdback shall be released to Artist upon: {{holdback_release_trigger}} (for example, "delivery of fully signed master-use and publishing clearance documents," "expiration of three years from initial release with no claim asserted," or "written agreement of the parties").

If clearance is unsuccessful and a third-party rights holder is granted a share of the recording or composition, the Holdback may be applied first against that share. Any unused Holdback shall be released to Artist within thirty (30) days of release of the trigger condition above.
