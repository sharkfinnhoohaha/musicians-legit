The name "{{band_name}}" (the "Band Name") is owned as follows: {{name_ownership_structure}}.

- "PARTNERSHIP" means the Band Name is a partnership asset owned jointly by all current Members in equal shares. If a Member leaves, that Member's interest in the Band Name is governed by the Leaving Member clause.
- "FOUNDING MEMBERS" means the Band Name is owned by the Founding Members listed here: {{founding_members}}. Members admitted later have a license to use the Band Name only while they are active Members.
- "LLC OR ENTITY" means the Band Name is owned by a separately formed entity, and Members hold their interest in the Band Name through their interest in that entity.

No Member may use the Band Name (or any confusingly similar variation) for any commercial musical project outside the Band without prior written consent of all other current Members.

Trademark filings, social-media handles, and domain registrations for the Band Name shall be held in the name of the owning party identified above. If any such filing or registration is currently held in an individual Member's name, that Member shall transfer it to the correct owner promptly upon request.
