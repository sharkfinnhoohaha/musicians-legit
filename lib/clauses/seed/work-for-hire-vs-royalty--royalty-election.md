If the parties have NOT elected work-for-hire treatment, Contributor's compensation shall be a royalty equal to {{royalty_pct}}% of {{royalty_base}} (e.g., net receipts, gross revenue, or distributor net) actually received by the Company from the exploitation of the Work.

Royalties shall be calculated and paid on a {{accounting_period}} basis (e.g., quarterly or semi-annually), within forty-five (45) days after the close of each accounting period. Each payment shall be accompanied by a statement showing units, gross revenue, deductions, and net royalty due.

Contributor may, on no more than one (1) occasion per twelve-month period and on at least thirty (30) days' written notice, audit Company's books and records relating to the Work at Contributor's expense, except that if an audit reveals an underpayment of more than five percent (5%) for the period audited, Company shall reimburse Contributor's reasonable audit costs.

Under this royalty alternative, Contributor retains authorship of the Work and any associated copyright interests, except for the specific use rights granted to Company elsewhere in this Agreement.
