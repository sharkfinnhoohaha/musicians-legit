The Writers agree that ownership of the Composition is divided as follows. The total of all shares listed below must equal one hundred percent (100%) on each side.

- {{party_a_legal_name}}: {{party_a_share_pct}}% Songwriter Share and {{party_a_share_pct}}% Publisher Share. Affiliated with {{party_a_pro}} (IPI/CAE: {{party_a_ipi}}).
- {{party_b_legal_name}}: {{party_b_share_pct}}% Songwriter Share and {{party_b_share_pct}}% Publisher Share. Affiliated with {{party_b_pro}} (IPI/CAE: {{party_b_ipi}}).

Each Writer is responsible for registering their own share of the Composition with their own performing rights organization (PRO) within thirty (30) days of the date of this Agreement. Each Writer agrees to promptly share with the other Writer the registration confirmation, including any work ID assigned by the PRO.

If a Writer changes PRO affiliation or assigns their Publisher Share to a third-party publisher, that Writer shall notify the other Writer in writing within fifteen (15) days so that the registrations can be kept consistent.
