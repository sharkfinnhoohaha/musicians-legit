{{artist_legal_name}} ("Artist") grants {{company_legal_name}} ("Company") an income participation in the following revenue streams (the "Included Streams"), and only the following streams: {{included_streams}}.

Common Included Streams that may be selected include: recorded-music net receipts, publishing income, touring net profits, merchandise net profits, sponsorship and brand-deal net fees, sync licensing, and name-and-likeness licensing. Each Included Stream must be specifically listed; nothing is included by implication.

Carve-Outs (the "Excluded Streams"): The following revenue streams are expressly excluded from Company's participation: {{carveouts}}. Common carve-outs include pre-existing songs, session-musician fees, acting and modeling income unrelated to Artist's music career, teaching income, and personal investment income.

Company's percentage and recoupment treatment for each Included Stream shall be set out in a separate schedule. Different streams may have different percentages and different recoupment rules. Income from Excluded Streams shall not flow through Company's accounting or be subject to recoupment.
