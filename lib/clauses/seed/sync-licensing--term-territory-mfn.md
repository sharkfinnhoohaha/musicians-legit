Term. The license granted in this Agreement is effective for {{license_term}} (e.g., "in perpetuity," "five (5) years," or "the life of the Production plus five years"). Unless otherwise stated, the term begins on the first public exhibition or release of the Production.

Territory. The license is valid in {{territory}} (e.g., "the Universe," "the United States and its territories," or a specific list of countries).

Most-Favored-Nations (MFN). Election: {{mfn_election}}.

- If "YES", the license fee of ${{license_fee}} is granted on a most-favored-nations basis with respect to any other music licensed for the Production. If Licensee pays a higher fee, or grants more favorable terms (term, territory, media), to any other music rights holder for use of comparable scope in the same Production, Licensee shall promptly notify Licensor and adjust this license to match the most favorable terms.
- If "NO", the fee and terms of this license stand alone and are not adjusted based on terms granted to other rights holders.

If a fee adjustment is required under MFN, Licensee shall pay the difference within thirty (30) days of identifying the more favorable terms.
