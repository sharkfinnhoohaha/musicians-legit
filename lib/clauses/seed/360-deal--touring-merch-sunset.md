Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, {{company_legal_name}}'s ("Company") participation in Artist's touring net profits and merchandise net profits shall sunset (end) {{touring_merch_sunset_years}} years after the end of the Term of this Agreement.

During the {{touring_merch_sunset_years}}-year sunset period, Company's percentage participation in touring and merchandise shall step down to {{post_term_step_down_pct}}% of the percentage in effect during the Term, applied to net profits only.

After the sunset period ends, Company shall have no further interest in Artist's touring or merchandise income, regardless of whether the underlying tour was first booked or first announced during the Term.

This sunset applies only to touring and merchandise. Recorded-music and publishing-income participations are governed separately and do not automatically sunset on the same schedule.
