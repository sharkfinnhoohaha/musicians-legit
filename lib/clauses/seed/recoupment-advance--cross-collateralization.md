The parties acknowledge that cross-collateralization is a structure under which {{company_legal_name}} ("Company") may recover unrecouped costs from one project, album, or revenue stream out of {{artist_legal_name}}'s ("Artist") earnings from a different project, album, or revenue stream.

Election: {{cross_collateralization_election}}.

- If "NONE", each project or revenue stream stands alone for recoupment purposes. Unrecouped balances on one project may not be recovered from earnings of another project.
- If "WITHIN ALBUM CYCLE", recoupment may be applied across recordings within a single album cycle (album plus singles released to support that album) but not across separate album cycles.
- If "ALL DEALS", recoupment may be applied across every project, advance, and revenue stream covered by this Agreement, including merchandise, touring, sync, and publishing income.

Company shall clearly identify on each royalty statement which costs have been applied against which revenue streams, so that Artist can verify the cross-collateralization scope elected above is being honored.
