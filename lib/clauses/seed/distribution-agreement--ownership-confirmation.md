{{artist_legal_name}} ("Artist") retains 100% ownership of the master sound recordings and the underlying compositions delivered to {{distributor_legal_name}} ("Distributor") under this Agreement. Distributor acquires no ownership interest in the recordings, compositions, artwork, name, or likeness, and acquires only the limited license to distribute as described in this Agreement.

Distributor's fee is {{distributor_fee_pct}}% of net receipts collected from digital service providers and other distribution channels. The remaining net receipts are paid through to Artist on the accounting schedule stated elsewhere in this Agreement.

Distributor shall not (a) sub-license the recordings to any record label, sync library, or sample pack, (b) include the recordings in any compilation, playlist for hire, or branded program, (c) negotiate or accept any settlement on Artist's behalf relating to the recordings, or (d) assign this Agreement to any third party, without Artist's prior written consent in each instance.

Distributor confirms that Artist's content shall be released, modified, or removed only on Artist's instruction or as required to respond to a documented third-party rights claim, and Distributor shall promptly notify Artist of any such claim.
