{{writer_legal_name}} ("Writer") engages {{administrator_legal_name}} ("Administrator") on an administration-only basis. Writer retains 100% ownership of the underlying compositions, including 100% of both the Songwriter Share and the Publisher Share. No copyright is assigned to Administrator under this election.

Administrator is granted the limited right, during the Term, to: (a) register the compositions with PROs, mechanical rights agencies, and digital service providers, (b) issue licenses on behalf of Writer at rates consistent with industry custom or as Writer pre-approves, (c) collect royalties on Writer's behalf, and (d) audit licensees on Writer's behalf.

Administrator's fee is {{admin_fee_pct}}% of the gross publishing income Administrator collects on Writer's behalf. The remaining {{admin_fee_pct}}% (correctly: 100 minus the admin fee percentage) is paid through to Writer.

The Term is {{term_years}} years from the effective date. After the Term ends, Administrator's right to issue new licenses ceases immediately. Administrator may continue to collect on licenses Administrator issued during the Term for a tail period of up to twelve (12) months, after which all collection rights revert fully to Writer with no further obligation owed to Administrator.
