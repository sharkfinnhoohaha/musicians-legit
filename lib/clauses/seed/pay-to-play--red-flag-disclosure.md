The parties acknowledge that this engagement is structured as a "pay-to-play" or "ticket-buyout" arrangement, in which {{artist_legal_name}} ("Artist") is required or expected to sell tickets in exchange for stage time. Artist enters this arrangement with full disclosure of the following terms.

Artist's ticket commitment is {{ticket_buyout_units}} tickets at a face price of ${{ticket_face_price}} per ticket. Artist is not required to purchase tickets to satisfy this commitment; Artist may meet the commitment by selling tickets to third parties.

If Artist sells more than the committed number of tickets, Artist shall receive {{artist_share_above_buyout_pct}}% of the face value of each additional ticket sold attributable to Artist. Tickets shall be tracked with a unique sale code or comparable mechanism so that attribution to Artist is verifiable.

{{promoter_legal_name}} ("Promoter") represents that: (a) the venue is a legitimate, licensed venue, (b) Artist is not personally liable for unsold tickets unless this Agreement explicitly says so, (c) Promoter is not charging Artist any production fee, sound fee, lighting fee, or "show fee" that has not been disclosed in writing, and (d) Artist has the right to cancel without penalty if any of the foregoing representations is materially inaccurate. Any obligation to "make up" unsold tickets in cash must be expressly initialed by Artist or it is void.
