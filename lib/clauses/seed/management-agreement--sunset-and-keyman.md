Sunset. After the Term ends, Manager shall continue to receive commission on income from contracts substantially negotiated during the Term, but only on a stepped-down basis for {{sunset_years}} years. The step-down schedule is: {{sunset_step_down_schedule}} (for example, 100% in year 1 of the sunset, 75% in year 2, 50% in year 3, then 0%). After the sunset period ends, no further commission is owed regardless of when the underlying contract was first negotiated.

Sunset does not apply to (a) renewals, extensions, or new options on contracts that are negotiated after the Term ends, (b) new agreements with parties Manager did not introduce, or (c) income from albums or works created entirely after the Term ends.

Key-Person. Artist's engagement of {{manager_legal_name}} is materially dependent on the personal services of {{key_person_name}} (the "Key Person"). If the Key Person dies, becomes incapacitated for more than ninety (90) consecutive days, or ceases to be actively involved in managing Artist's career, Artist may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice without penalty. Sunset commissions accrued before such termination remain payable on the stepped-down schedule above.
