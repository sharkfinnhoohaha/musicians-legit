The parties intend that the contribution by {{contributor_legal_name}} ("Contributor") to {{work_description}} (the "Work") be treated as a "work made for hire" commissioned by {{commissioning_party_legal_name}} ("Company").

For a specially commissioned work made for hire to be effective in the United States, the work must fall within one of the categories enumerated by the US Copyright Office in Circular 30. The parties confirm that the Work falls within the following category: {{wfh_category}} (e.g., contribution to a collective work, part of an audiovisual work, compilation, instructional text, or other listed category).

If for any reason the Work does not qualify as a work made for hire, Contributor hereby assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in the Work, including all copyrights, as a backup transfer. This backup assignment takes effect automatically upon delivery of the Work without any further action by either party.

Contributor acknowledges that under a work-for-hire arrangement, Contributor is not the author of the Work for copyright purposes and is not entitled to royalties from exploitation of the Work unless separately stated in writing.
