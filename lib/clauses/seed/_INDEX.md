# Clause Seed Index

Total clauses: 34. Each file is one atomic, reusable clause with YAML frontmatter and a markdown body using `{{handlebar}}` placeholders.

All clauses sourced from public-domain US Copyright Office circulars, USPTO public guidance, US Courts public information, and paraphrased general music-business conventions from publicly available educational materials. No verbatim copying from copyrighted attorney-drafted templates.

`lawyer_reviewed: false` on every clause — these are templates, not legal advice.

## Clauses by Archetype

### split-sheet
- `split-sheet--definitions` — Defines the parties, the Composition, Songwriter Share, and Publisher Share, and clarifies that the split sheet covers composition only (not master).
- `split-sheet--ownership-percentages` — Sets writer percentages, PRO affiliations, IPI/CAE numbers, and registration responsibility within 30 days.
- `split-sheet--master-vs-publishing` — Explicitly distinguishes underlying composition copyright from sound recording (master) copyright; prevents implied transfer of master rights.

### producer-beat-agreement
- `producer-beat-agreement--lease-vs-exclusive` — Forces an explicit election between non-exclusive lease (with unit cap) and exclusive license.
- `producer-beat-agreement--master-points-publishing` — Documents producer master points and publishing share, treating them as independent ongoing economic interests.
- `producer-beat-agreement--sample-warranty` — Producer warrants the beat is original; requires disclosure of any third-party material; producer indemnifies for breach. (Also applies to sample-clearance.)

### work-for-hire-vs-royalty
- `work-for-hire-vs-royalty--wfh-election` — WFH election with US Copyright Office Circular 30 statutory category check, plus backup assignment if WFH fails.
- `work-for-hire-vs-royalty--royalty-election` — Royalty alternative with %, base, accounting period, and audit rights.

### recoupment-advance
- `recoupment-advance--cross-collateralization` — Forces an explicit election among NONE / WITHIN ALBUM CYCLE / ALL DEALS, with statement-level disclosure obligation.
- `recoupment-advance--recoupable-costs` — Lists what is recoupable from artist royalties vs what the company eats; pre-approval gate for anything outside the list.

### 360-deal
- `360-deal--scope-with-carveouts` — Enumerates included revenue streams and explicit carve-outs; nothing included by implication.
- `360-deal--touring-merch-sunset` — Sunset on touring/merch participation X years after term ends, with step-down percentage.

### management-agreement
- `management-agreement--commission-base` — Forces gross vs net election; lists pre-deduction items; carves out refunds and unrecouped advances.
- `management-agreement--sunset-and-keyman` — Sunset on post-term commissions with stepped schedule, plus key-person termination right.

### sync-licensing
- `sync-licensing--one-stop-grant` — One-stop grant for both composition and master with fallback if licensor doesn't actually control 100%.
- `sync-licensing--term-territory-mfn` — Term, territory, and MFN election with adjustment mechanic.

### sample-clearance
- `sample-clearance--who-clears` — Allocates clearance responsibility to a specific party with deadline and remedy ladder.
- `sample-clearance--royalty-holdback` — Royalty hold-back % held in reserve until clearance complete or trigger met.

### band-partnership
- `band-partnership--name-ownership` — Band name owned via partnership / founding members / LLC; trademark and handle holding rules.
- `band-partnership--leaving-member` — Voting thresholds (routine vs major), buyout formula, and pre/post-departure song ownership including re-recording.

### performance-gig
- `performance-gig--deposit-and-payment` — Deposit %, due date, balance timing; non-payment consequences.
- `performance-gig--cancellation-and-force-majeure` — Sliding cancellation ladder, artist-side cancellation rules, and force majeure with reschedule preference.

### pay-to-play
- `pay-to-play--red-flag-disclosure` — Disclosure-mode clause that protects artist when pay-to-play is unavoidable: ticket cap, attribution, no hidden fees.
- `pay-to-play--no-commitment-default` — Affirmative warranty that there is NO pay-to-play obligation unless separately initialed; cancellation right if violated. (Also applies to performance-gig.)

### publishing-admin-vs-copub
- `publishing-admin-vs-copub--admin-only` — Admin-only election: writer keeps 100% ownership, admin gets a fee and limited rights, with clean reversion at end of term.
- `publishing-admin-vs-copub--copub-election` — Co-publishing election: partial assignment of publisher share; songwriter share preserved; admin termination for chronic mis-accounting.

### distribution-agreement
- `distribution-agreement--term-and-exclusivity` — Term length, exclusivity election, takedown timing, and post-term removal obligation.
- `distribution-agreement--ownership-confirmation` — Confirms artist retains 100% ownership; lists things distributor cannot do without consent.

### master-ownership
- `master-ownership--who-paid-test` — "Who paid for what" rule with explicit default election (artist-owns / cost-payer-owns / joint).
- `master-ownership--default-rule` — Defines Master and requires written ownership statement before first commercial release; SoundExchange registration coordination.

### nda-collab
- `nda-collab--scope-of-confidential-info` — Defines Confidential Information for music collaborations with the four standard exceptions and subpoena cooperation.
- `nda-collab--obligations-and-term` — Use restrictions, term in years, return-or-destroy window, trade-secret carve-out, no-poach.

### Universal boilerplate
- `boilerplate--governing-law-delaware` — Default Delaware governing law, mediation-first dispute resolution, arbitration vs court election. Applies to all 14 archetypes.
- `boilerplate--entire-agreement-severability` — Entire agreement, amendment, severability, waiver, electronic notice, counterparts. Applies to all 14 archetypes.
