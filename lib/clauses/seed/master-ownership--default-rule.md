"Master" means the sound recording titled "{{master_title}}" in its final mixed and mastered form, including all stems, multitracks, and pre-final mixes, regardless of file format or storage location.

The featured performing artists on the Master are: {{featured_artists}}. The credited producer is {{producer_credit}}.

In the absence of a separate written agreement assigning ownership, the parties agree that all owners of the Master (whether one party or several) shall be identified in writing by name and percentage on or before the first commercial release of the Master, and that this written ownership statement shall be the controlling document for purposes of registration, licensing, and revenue collection.

Each owner is responsible for registering their share with their preferred neighboring-rights or master-collection society (for example, SoundExchange in the United States) using the agreed ISRC and the agreed ownership splits. Inconsistent registrations shall be corrected by majority vote of the owners weighted by ownership percentage.
