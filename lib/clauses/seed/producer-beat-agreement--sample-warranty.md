{{producer_legal_name}} ("Producer") represents and warrants that the Beat is original to Producer and does not contain any uncleared samples, interpolations, replays, or other third-party material owned or controlled by anyone other than Producer.

If the Beat does contain any third-party material, Producer shall disclose in writing prior to delivery: (a) the source of the material, (b) the rights holders, and (c) whether clearance has been obtained. Producer shall not deliver any Beat containing undisclosed third-party material.

Producer agrees to indemnify and hold Artist harmless from any third-party claim arising directly from a breach of the warranty in this clause, limited to the direct damages, settlement amounts, and reasonable legal fees attributable to the uncleared material. This indemnity does not apply to material added by Artist or by anyone other than Producer after delivery of the Beat.
