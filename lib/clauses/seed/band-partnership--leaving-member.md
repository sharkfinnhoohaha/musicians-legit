Voting. Routine decisions (booking, set lists, day-to-day expenses) require a vote of {{vote_threshold_routine}} of current Members. Major decisions (signing a record, management, or publishing deal; admitting or removing a Member; selling Band assets; adopting a new Band Name) require {{vote_threshold_major}} of current Members.

Leaving Member. A Member may leave the Band by giving thirty (30) days' written notice. A Member may be removed for cause by the major-decision vote threshold above. A Leaving Member is bought out under the formula: {{buyout_formula}} (for example, "book value of Band assets divided by number of current Members" or "an independent valuation paid out over time"). The buyout shall be paid over no more than {{buyout_payment_period_months}} months from the effective date of departure.

Song Ownership Pre- and Post-Departure. A Leaving Member retains their existing songwriting and master ownership shares in songs released or substantially recorded before their departure, exactly as documented on the relevant split sheets and recording documents. A Leaving Member has no ownership interest in songs first written or first substantially recorded after their departure. The Leaving Member retains the right to perform live the songs they co-wrote, but does not have the right to re-record those songs commercially without consent of the remaining Members for the period of any applicable re-recording restriction.

A Leaving Member may not use the Band Name in any future project, must reasonably cooperate with transition (turning over passwords, files, and contacts), and continues to receive any contractual royalties already earned on their pre-departure work.
