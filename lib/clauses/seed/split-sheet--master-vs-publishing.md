The Writers acknowledge that two distinct copyrights may exist in connection with "{{song_working_title}}": (a) the copyright in the underlying musical composition (lyrics and music), and (b) the copyright in any sound recording (the "Master") that captures a performance of the Composition.

This Agreement divides ownership only of the Composition. Ownership of any Master is governed separately and is not granted, transferred, or implied by this Agreement.

Each Writer represents that nothing in this Agreement waives, limits, or transfers any interest they may have in any Master, and that any future agreement concerning a Master will be documented in a separate writing signed by the relevant parties.
