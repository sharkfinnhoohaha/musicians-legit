{{writer_legal_name}} ("Writer") and {{copublisher_legal_name}} ("Co-Publisher") elect a co-publishing structure. Writer retains 100% of the Songwriter Share. Writer assigns to Co-Publisher {{copub_share_pct}}% of the Publisher Share for the compositions covered by this Agreement.

Under this structure, the Songwriter Share continues to be paid directly to Writer through Writer's PRO and is not subject to administration fees. The Publisher Share is split such that Writer retains the remaining percentage of the Publisher Share and Co-Publisher receives {{copub_share_pct}}%.

The assignment to Co-Publisher is limited to the Publisher Share described above and does not include the Songwriter Share. Co-Publisher's interest in the Publisher Share lasts for the Term of this Agreement, which is {{term_years}} years, after which the assigned share reverts fully to Writer unless the parties agree in writing to extend.

Co-Publisher may administer the entire Publisher Share (100%) during the Term, including Writer's retained share, and shall account to Writer for Writer's portion. If Co-Publisher fails to provide accurate statements for two (2) consecutive accounting periods, Writer may terminate Co-Publisher's administration role on thirty (30) days' notice while Co-Publisher's underlying ownership share continues until the end of the Term.
