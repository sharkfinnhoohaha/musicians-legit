{{licensor_legal_name}} ("Licensor") represents that it controls 100% of the rights necessary to grant a synchronization license in both the underlying composition titled "{{composition_title}}" and the master sound recording titled "{{master_title}}" (together, the "Work"), and grants {{licensee_legal_name}} ("Licensee") a "one-stop" license covering both rights through this single Agreement.

Licensee may synchronize the Work in timed relation with the audiovisual production titled "{{production_title}}" (the "Production") and may distribute, exhibit, and promote the Production through the media listed here: {{media_scope}}.

If Licensor does not in fact control 100% of either the composition or the master, Licensor shall promptly identify any other rights holders so Licensee can negotiate directly. In that event, this license operates only on Licensor's actual share, and the fee shall be reduced pro rata.

This grant does not include the right to alter the lyrics or fundamental melody of the Work, to use the Work in a parody, or to use the Work as the title of the Production, unless such use is separately approved in writing by Licensor.
