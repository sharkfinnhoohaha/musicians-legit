The Receiving Party shall (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, (b) protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and not less than reasonable care, (c) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party except to its employees, contractors, or advisors who have a clear need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement, and (d) not use Confidential Information to compete with the Disclosing Party or to solicit any of the Disclosing Party's collaborators, employees, or business contacts.

The confidentiality obligations in this Agreement last for {{confidentiality_term_years}} years from the date of disclosure. Trade secrets shall remain protected for as long as they qualify as trade secrets under applicable law, even if longer than the stated term.

Within {{return_or_destroy_window_days}} days after the earlier of (i) completion of the Purpose, (ii) termination of any related collaboration, or (iii) written request by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all tangible and digital copies of Confidential Information in its possession or control, except for one archival copy that may be retained solely for legal compliance purposes and that remains subject to this Agreement.

Nothing in this Agreement transfers any ownership interest in any Confidential Information. All rights remain with the Disclosing Party.
