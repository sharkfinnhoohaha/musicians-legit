{{promoter_legal_name}} ("Promoter") represents and warrants that this engagement does NOT include any pay-to-play, ticket-buyout, or ticket-pre-sale obligation on {{artist_legal_name}} ("Artist") unless such obligation is expressly stated in a separate clause of this Agreement and signed or initialed by Artist.

Promoter further confirms that Artist is not required to purchase tickets, sell a minimum number of tickets, bring a minimum number of attendees, or pay any fee back to Promoter as a condition of performing.

If Promoter introduces or attempts to enforce any such obligation that is not expressly documented and signed, Artist may cancel the engagement without penalty, retain any deposit already received, and recover documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred in reliance on the engagement.

This clause is intended to make the absence of a pay-to-play obligation an explicit and verifiable promise by Promoter, not merely an unstated default.
