The parties acknowledge that the recording covered by this Agreement contains the following sample, interpolation, or replay: {{sample_description}} (the "Sample").

The Party responsible for clearing the Sample (the "Clearing Party") is: {{clearing_party}}. The Clearing Party shall, at its own cost, obtain in writing both (a) a master-use license from the owner of the original sound recording (if a recording is sampled) and (b) a synchronization or interpolation license from the owner of the underlying composition.

Clearance must be completed by {{clearance_deadline}}. If clearance is not obtained by that date, the parties shall, at the Clearing Party's election: (i) replay or replace the Sample with a cleared substitute, (ii) remove the Sample entirely, or (iii) delay release until clearance is obtained.

The Clearing Party shall provide the other Party with copies of all signed clearance documents at least seven (7) days before commercial release of the recording. No commercial release shall occur until both clearances are confirmed in writing.
