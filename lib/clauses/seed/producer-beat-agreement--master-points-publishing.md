In addition to any flat fee paid for the Beat, {{producer_legal_name}} ("Producer") shall receive the following ongoing economic interests in any commercially released recording that incorporates the Beat (each, a "Recording"):

- Master Points: {{master_points_pct}}% of net receipts from exploitation of the Recording, calculated on the same basis as the Artist's net receipts from the Recording (i.e., after distributor fees and recoupable costs identified in writing).
- Publishing Share: {{producer_publishing_share_pct}}% of the underlying composition embodied in the Recording, including the corresponding share of mechanical and performance royalties payable through the Producer's chosen PRO and publishing administrator.

Master Points and the Publishing Share are independent. Loss or reduction of one does not reduce the other. Producer is responsible for registering the Producer's Publishing Share with the Producer's PRO and publishing administrator.

If the Recording is unreleased or not commercially exploited, no Master Points or Publishing Share are owed, but Producer's underlying ownership interests are not waived.
